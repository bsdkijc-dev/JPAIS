**Project Owner:** Indonesia Judicial Center (IJC) | **Lead:** Cecep Mustafa | **Date:** 2026-11-12

## 1. Executive Reconciliation Summary
- **January 2026 Operational Satker Baseline:** **923** court units (416 PU + 446 PA + 38 TUN + 23 MIL), excluding 7 central administrative units (930 total satkers).
- **July 2026 Military Court Expansion (SK 54A/SEK/SK.KU1.1.1/VII/2026):** **+5** military court units (Dilmilti IV Balikpapan, Dilmilti V Makassar, Dilmil I-03 Pekanbaru, Dilmil V-18 Kendari, Dilmil V-21 Manokwari) → **928** operational satkers (935 total satkers).
- **December 2025 Presidential Decrees ([Keppres 39/2025](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/343566/keppres-no-39-tahun-2025-pembentukan-pengadilan-negeri-kabupaten-badung-pengadil), [Keppres 40/2025](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/343592/keppres-no-40-tahun-2025-pembentukan-pengadilan-agama-kabupaten-kepulauan-sula-p), [Keppres 41/2025](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/343593/keppres-no-41-tahun-2025-pembentukan-pengadilan-tata-usaha-negara-tanjung-selor)):** **+24** newly established courts (13 PN + 9 PA + 2 PTUN) not yet reflected in the Jan 2026 DIPA/KPA satker list.
